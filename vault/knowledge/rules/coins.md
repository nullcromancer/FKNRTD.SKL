# Coin recognition

- Visually record denomination, country/issuer, date, mint mark, legends, design, metal/color appearance, edge and visible damage. A photograph rarely proves composition, weight, authenticity, or grade.
- Separate type identification from date/mint variety. Verify date and mint mark at sufficient crop resolution; if unclear, keep the identity broad and queue review.
- Use authoritative mint or catalog references for identity and specialist auction/completed-sale evidence for value. Match denomination, date, mint, variety, certification, and grade where possible. Do not use melt value without supported composition and a current metal price source.
- Never present an estimated grade as certification. Note glare, wear, scale, and image limitations.
