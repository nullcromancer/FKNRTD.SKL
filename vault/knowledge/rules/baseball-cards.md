# Baseball card recognition

- Read the player name, team, year, manufacturer, set, card number, and visible variation independently from the crop. Confirm with a published set checklist or reliable catalog before assigning an exact identity.
- For older issues, distinguish reprints, regional issues, cabinet/strip cards, promotional cards, and modern reproductions by typography, dimensions, back design, paper, and catalog evidence. A visually similar auction record is not automatically a comparable.
- Record front/back evidence separately when both sides are intentionally photographed. Never infer a back number from a player name alone.
- Search Google and eBay first, then reputable checklist databases and auction archives. Prefer sold records matching issue, pose/variation, grade, and condition. Preserve the actual page URL and identify the price basis.
